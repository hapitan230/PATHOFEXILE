Juggernaut

The Juggernaut is the premier tank class, with access to Endurance Charge generation at minimal effort and additional benefits per charge, alongside hefty boosts to armour, strong life regeneration perks, and protection against slows and stuns.

While focused on defence, the Juggernaut can also increase both attack speed and accuracy by increasing strength, resulting in a nigh-indestructible yet fast-hitting character.
